# Rustic Revived #
Rustic Revived is an unofficial port of [Rustic](https://github.com/cadaverous-eris/Rustic) by cadaverous-eris to **NeoForge 1.21.1** and **26.1.2**. This wiki covers the **1.21.1** version. Rustic is a mod that adds many medieval-themed features to Minecraft.

In 1.21.1 and newer, every block, item, fluid and recipe lives in the `rusticrevived` namespace (for example `rusticrevived:olive_log`), while the mod id is `rustic`. JEI finds the mod's items with both `@rustic` and `@rusticrevived`.
