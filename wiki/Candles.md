Candles are decorative light sources made from iron, string, and either [beeswax](Bees.md) or [tallow](Materials.md). They can be placed on the sides or top of solid blocks, just like torches.

![](http://i.imgur.com/oaLB0Dw.png)

## Variants ##
+ Candles come in iron, gold and silver versions. Silver candles need a mod that adds silver ingots.
+ **Candelabras** hold two candles and are crafted with string, wax and an ingot.
+ **Candle Levers** are crafted with a candle and redstone. Right-clicking one toggles a redstone signal, like a vanilla lever.
