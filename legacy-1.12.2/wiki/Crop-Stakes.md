Crop Stakes can be made with any wooden planks. They are used in the cultivation of [chili peppers](https://github.com/the-realest-stu/Rustic/wiki/Chili-Peppers) and [tomatoes](https://github.com/the-realest-stu/Rustic/wiki/Tomatoes). [Ropes](https://github.com/the-realest-stu/Rustic/wiki/Rope) can also be tied to crop stakes.
