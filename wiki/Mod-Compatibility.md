Rustic Revived works on its own, but it integrates with several popular mods when they are installed. No addons are required.

***

## Dynamic Trees ##
Rustic is fully compatible with [Dynamic Trees](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/dynamictrees) right out of the box. When Dynamic Trees is installed, Rustic's [olive](Olives.md) and [ironwood](Ironberries.md) trees are made dynamic, and Rustic's [apple trees](Apple-Trees.md) are replaced with the ones from Dynamic Trees itself. The integration can be turned off with the `enableDynamicTreesCompat` config option.

## JEI ##
[JEI](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jei) shows every crushing tub, evaporating basin, alchemy and brewing recipe, as well as the special crafting recipes (olive oiling, vanta oiling, bottle and barrel emptying). Searching `@rustic` or `@rusticrevived` lists all of the mod's items.

## CraftTweaker and KubeJS ##
Modpack makers can add and remove recipes for all of Rustic's machines. See [CraftTweaker Support](CraftTweaker-Support.md) (1.21.1 only) and [KubeJS Support](KubeJS-Support.md).

## Quark ##
In 1.21.1, [Quark](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/quark)'s inventory buttons (sort, deposit, restock...) are added to Rustic's storage blocks: [cabinets](Cabinets.md), [pots](Pots.md) and [barrels](Barrels.md).

## Inventory Tweaks Refoxed ##
Rustic's storage blocks can be sorted with [Inventory Tweaks Refoxed](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/inventory-tweaks-refoxed).

## Other Stone Types ##
[Limestone and marble pillars](Stone-Pillars.md) can be crafted when another mod adds items to the `c:stones/limestone` or `c:stones/marble` tags, and the [silver decorations](Candles.md) need a mod that adds silver ingots (`c:ingots/silver`).
