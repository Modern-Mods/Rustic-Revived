# Rustic #
Rustic is a mod that adds many medieval-themed features to Minecraft.

***

## Downloads ##
All versions can be found on [curseforge](https://minecraft.curseforge.com/projects/rustic)

## Feature Suggestions ##
If you have a suggestion for something I should add or change about the mod, create an issue [here](https://github.com/the-realest-stu/Rustic/issues). There is no guarantee that your ideas will be implemented, but I whether I decide to add your ideas or not, I will try to respond to the issue and explain my decision.
