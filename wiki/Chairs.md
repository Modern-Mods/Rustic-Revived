Chairs can be crafted with sticks wooden planks. The planks used in crafting affect the type of chair you get. Chairs can be made with any vanilla wood types, as well as [olive wood](Olives.md) and [ironwood](Ironberries.md). Their sole purpose is decoration. Right-clicking a chair will allow you to sit in it, and sneaking will allow you to dismount.
