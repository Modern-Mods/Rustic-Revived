Lanterns can be crafted with iron and coal, and they emit light. They can be placed on the sides, top, or bottom of any block.

![](http://i.imgur.com/2hTNYx3.png)
## Variants ##
+ Iron, golden and silver lanterns (silver lanterns need a mod that adds silver ingots).
+ **Wooden Lanterns** are crafted with sticks, planks, glass panes and a torch.
+ **Iron Torches** are crafted with an iron nugget, coal and a stick.
