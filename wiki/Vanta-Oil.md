Vanta Oil is used to coat weapons with the effects of [elixirs](Alchemy.md) and potions.

***

## Vanta Lily ##
Vanta Lilies spawn naturally in plains and forest biomes. They can be [crushed](Crushing-Tub.md) into Vanta Oil (250 mB per lily), which can be bottled or collected with a bucket.

## Oiling Weapons ##
Vanta Oil can be crafted with most melee weapons, along with any number of elixirs or potions of the same type, to imbue the weapon with the effect of those elixirs or potions. Striking another creature with an oiled weapon uses some of the weapon's oil to apply the imbued effect onto the target. The weapon's tooltip shows the imbued effect and how many uses remain.

Effects that are not instant are applied for about 15 seconds per hit, and subsequent blows add on to the duration of the applied effect. You can add more oil to an already oiled weapon by crafting it with more Vanta Oil and more elixirs or potions with the same effect.

The items that can be oiled can be changed with the `vantaOilWhitelist` config option.
