There are a few items in Rustic which are used solely as crafting ingredients, and are not mentioned on other pages of the wiki.

***

## Items ##
+ Tallow
    - Obtained by smelting rotten flesh.
    - Can be used to craft [candles](Candles.md).
    - Can be disabled in the mod's config file.
+ Tiny Pile of Iron Dust
    - Obtained by [evaporating](Evaporating-Basin.md) [ironberry juice](Ironberries.md).
    - Can be smelted into an iron nugget.
+ Gold Dust
    - Obtained by [evaporating](Evaporating-Basin.md) golden apple juice.
    - Can be crafted into a gold ingot.
