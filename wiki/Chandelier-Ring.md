Chandelier Rings can be crafted with iron and [chain](Chain.md). They are most useful for decoration, as torches and [candles](Candles.md) can be placed on the sides of the block. For the best looking chandeliers, it is suggested that you hang the chandelier ring from a chain or [rope](Rope.md).
