Stone pillars can be crafted from stone, andesite, diorite, granite, polished basalt and [slate](Slate.md). Limestone and marble pillars can also be crafted when another mod adds limestone or marble (`c:stones/limestone` and `c:stones/marble` tags). They can be placed along any of the three axes.
