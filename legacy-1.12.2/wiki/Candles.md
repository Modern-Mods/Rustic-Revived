Candles are decorative light sources made from iron, string, and either [beeswax](https://github.com/the-realest-stu/Rustic/wiki/Bees) or [tallow](https://github.com/the-realest-stu/Rustic/wiki/Materials). They can be placed on the sides or top of solid blocks, just like torches.
